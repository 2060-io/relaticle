Once your Google or Microsoft account is connected, your calendar events
sync into Relaticle as meetings. You see your day on **Home**, and each record shows
the meetings that involve it.

## See your day

The **Meetings** panel on **Home** lists your meetings for today. Use the
date control to look at another day. It is your own calendar: it shows
meetings from your connected accounts and meetings you are invited to.

## See a record's meetings

Open a person, company, or opportunity and click its **Meetings** tab. Each
row shows the title, the time, the attendees, the status, and **My RSVP**.

Relaticle links a meeting to a person when an attendee's address matches
that person's email, and to a company by the attendee's domain.

![A person's Meetings tab showing one confirmed meeting with its time, attendees, and an Accepted RSVP](/help-assets/email-and-calendar/see-meetings-and-link-them-to-records-1.png)

Open a meeting from its row menu. You see the **Participants**, the
**Linked records**, the **Description**, and **Open in calendar**, which
opens the event in Google Calendar or Outlook.

## Link a meeting to another record

A meeting about an opportunity often has no attendee Relaticle can match.
Link it yourself.

1. Open the meeting and click **Link records**, or choose
   **Link to record** in the meeting's row menu.
2. Choose the **Type**: person, company, or opportunity.
3. Choose the **Record** and confirm.

**Unlink from this record** removes the meeting from the record you are on.
The event stays on your calendar.

## Answer an invitation

Open the meeting. The button beside its title shows your current answer.
Click it and choose **Accept**, **Maybe**, or **Decline**. Relaticle sends
the answer to your Google or Outlook calendar. Declining leaves the meeting
on the calendar for the other guests.

## If your RSVP could not be updated

- **Reconnect the mailbox and try again** means Google or Microsoft stopped
  accepting the saved access. Reconnect the account under **Workspace Settings**,
  **Email and Calendar**.
- **This meeting has not appeared on your calendar yet** means the event is
  still syncing. Try again in a moment.

## If a record shows Nothing to show here

The record's address is protected or blocked in your workspace's
**Email visibility** settings, so its emails and meetings stay hidden.
